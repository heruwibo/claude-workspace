# Deloitte-style slide deck

Style inspired by publicly visible conventions of Big Four advisory decks. Do not use any firm's logo or trademarks; the look is emulated, not branded.

## Storyline
- Structured and methodical: context → approach → findings → recommendations → roadmap → governance.
- More descriptive than strategy-boutique decks: it is normal to include scope, methodology, assumptions and detailed findings for traceability.
- Typical flow: Title → Agenda → Executive summary → Background and scope → Approach → Findings by theme → Recommendations → Implementation roadmap → Risks and governance → Next steps → Appendix.

## Slide anatomy
- **Title**: clear statement of the finding or topic, left-aligned, 1–2 lines; a short descriptive subtitle is acceptable.
- Agenda/section divider slides with large numbers and a dark background.
- Body: structured layouts: columns, tables, process chevrons, maturity models, heat maps, RACI matrices.
- Footer: source/notes, confidentiality line, slide number.

## Visual language
- Black and white base with a vivid **green** accent (~#86BC25) and dark/mid greys; teal or blue as secondary data colors.
- Clean sans-serif, title ~24–28 pt, body 12–14 pt.
- Dark divider slides, light content slides; consistent grid and margins.
- Charts: simple bars/columns, doughnuts used sparingly, maturity spider/radar charts, risk heat maps (red/amber/green).
- Tables are common; keep them clean with light row rules and bold header row.

## Rules of thumb
- Make traceability explicit: each finding links to evidence and a recommendation.
- Use RAG status and maturity levels with a legend defined on the slide.
- Roadmaps: phases as chevrons with workstreams as swim lanes, durations, owners.
- Include assumptions, scope exclusions and limitations where relevant.
- Keep to 3–5 key points per slide; push detail to the appendix.

## Workflow
1. Confirm scope, audience (board, steering committee, working team) and deliverable type.
2. Outline sections and agenda; draft executive summary.
3. Build slides (pptx skill for .pptx) and check legends, sources, consistent terminology and a coherent roadmap.
