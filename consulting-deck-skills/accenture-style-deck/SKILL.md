# Accenture-style slide deck

Style inspired by publicly visible conventions of technology-consulting decks. Do not use any firm's logo or trademarks; the look is emulated, not branded.

## Storyline
- Transformation narrative: why change now → vision → capabilities needed → solution/architecture → journey and roadmap → value case.
- Mixes strategy with delivery: include target operating model, tech architecture, and phased delivery plan.
- Typical flow: Title → Executive summary → Market forces and challenges → Vision and ambition → Solution and architecture → Use cases/journeys → Roadmap and waves → Value and business case → Team, governance, next steps.

## Slide anatomy
- **Title**: forward-looking statement, 1–2 lines; confident, active language ("Reinvent the customer journey with an AI-enabled platform").
- Hero/statement slides with large type and a single idea.
- Body: layered architecture diagrams, journey maps, capability maps, swim-lane roadmaps, value-driver trees.
- Footer: source, confidentiality, slide number.

## Visual language
- Black, white and **purple** (~#A100FF, with deeper #7500C0 as a secondary) on mostly white or black backgrounds; greys for structure.
- Modern sans-serif, strong size contrast between headline and body (title 28+ pt, body 12–14 pt).
- Flat icons, rounded shapes, chevrons and arrows; occasional full-bleed dark slides for emphasis.
- Diagrams: layered stack (experience / apps / data / infrastructure), customer journey with touchpoints and pain points, wave-based roadmaps, capability heat maps.
- Charts: simple bars/lines with purple highlight and grey context.

## Rules of thumb
- Show the "what it looks like": a concrete architecture, journey or use case beats abstract bullets.
- Tie every technology element to a business outcome and metric.
- Roadmaps use waves (e.g., Wave 1 quick wins, Wave 2 scale, Wave 3 optimize) with dependencies noted.
- Business case: costs, benefits, payback, and key assumptions on one slide.

## Workflow
1. Clarify business goal, current-state constraints, audience and technology scope.
2. Outline the story from drivers to roadmap to value.
3. Build slides (pptx skill for .pptx); check each diagram is legible, each tech element maps to an outcome, and the roadmap is consistent with the value case.
