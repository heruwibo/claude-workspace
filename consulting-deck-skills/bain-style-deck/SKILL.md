# Bain-style slide deck

Style inspired by publicly visible conventions of strategy-firm decks. Do not use any firm's logo or trademarks; the look is emulated, not branded.

## Storyline
- Results-first: lead with the answer and the quantified impact ("what it's worth").
- Strong "true north" and action orientation: every section ends with what to do and by when.
- Typical flow: Executive summary → Situation → Diagnostic → Recommendation with sized impact → Implementation plan, owners, KPIs → Risks → Appendix.

## Slide anatomy
- **Title**: concise full-sentence conclusion, 1–2 lines, usually dark grey/black text.
- Body: one dominant chart or table; simple callouts with a bold number ("+$120M EBITDA").
- Takeaway bar or boxed message at the bottom or right.
- Footer: source, notes, page number; small, grey.

## Visual language
- White background, black/charcoal text, **red** (~#CC0000) as the single strong accent, with greys; sparing use of a secondary color for comparisons.
- Plain sans-serif (Arial/Helvetica-like), title ~24 pt, body 12–14 pt.
- Minimal decoration: thin lines, flat fills, no 3D, no shadows.
- Charts: bars, waterfalls (bridges from current to target), line charts for trends; highlight the key series in red, others grey; direct labels, gridlines removed.
- Common tools: customer/NPS views, value-at-stake bridges, 90-day plans, initiative heat maps.

## Rules of thumb
- Quantify everything possible: baseline, target, delta, timeline.
- Separate facts (what the data shows) from judgment (what we recommend).
- Implementation slides name owners, milestones and KPIs; no vague verbs.
- Prefer a bridge/waterfall to explain how you get from A to B.

## Workflow
1. Clarify the objective, the metric that matters and the audience.
2. Draft titles as a results story with sized impact.
3. Build visuals (pptx skill for .pptx) and check numbers reconcile across slides and every chart has a source.
