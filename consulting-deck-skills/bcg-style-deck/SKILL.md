# BCG-style slide deck

Style inspired by publicly visible conventions of strategy-firm decks. Do not use any firm's logo or trademarks; the look is emulated, not branded.

## Storyline
- Hypothesis-driven: state the point of view up front, then prove it with evidence.
- Emphasis on conceptual frameworks and "big ideas" (growth-share-type matrices, value-creation levers, archetypes) that make the logic memorable.
- Typical flow: Key takeaways → Situation and ambition → Insights/diagnostic → Strategic options → Recommended path → Implementation and impact → Appendix.

## Slide anatomy
- **Headline**: full-sentence insight, 1–2 lines, left-aligned, sentence case.
- Optional subhead / chart title in grey beneath the headline describing the metric ("Revenue by segment, 2020–25, $B").
- Body: framework or chart on the left/center, a **key takeaway box** (shaded panel with 2–3 short points) on the right or bottom.
- Footer: "Source: …" and "Note: …", slide number, small.

## Visual language
- White background; dark green (~#175E54) as the anchor, brighter green (~#29BA74) as accent, with greys and occasionally a secondary teal/blue.
- Clean sans-serif; title ~24 pt, body 12–14 pt.
- Rounded or clean-edged shaded boxes for takeaways; arrows and chevrons for process flows.
- Charts: bar, stacked bar, bubble charts, and 2x2 matrices; highlight with green, mute rest in grey; label data directly.
- Icons: simple line icons are acceptable to anchor levers or archetypes.

## Rules of thumb
- Each slide = one insight + proof + "so what".
- Use frameworks to simplify (3–5 archetypes, 2x2 axes with clear low/high labels), not to decorate.
- Quantify: impact ranges, sizing, and timelines on options slides.
- Options slides compare alternatives against consistent criteria (Harvey balls or ratings).

## Workflow
1. Define the client question, audience and decision.
2. Choose 1–2 signature frameworks that carry the story.
3. Write headlines first, then build visuals (pptx skill for .pptx). Check each slide has a takeaway and a source.
