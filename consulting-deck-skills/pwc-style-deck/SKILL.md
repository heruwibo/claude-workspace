# PwC-style slide deck

Style inspired by publicly visible conventions of Big Four advisory decks. Do not use any firm's logo or trademarks; the look is emulated, not branded.

## Storyline
- Practical and client-outcome oriented: challenge → our view → what it means → what to do → how to get there.
- Balances narrative with structured detail; frequently includes benchmarks, survey insights and "key takeaways".
- Typical flow: Title → Agenda → Executive summary → Context and key trends → Diagnostic/findings → Recommendations or options → Roadmap and business case → Next steps → Appendix.

## Slide anatomy
- **Title**: clear statement of the key message, 1–2 lines, left-aligned, sentence case.
- Optional subheading in a lighter weight or accent color.
- Body: structured columns, numbered lists, process chevrons, comparison tables, stat callouts ("68% of CEOs…"), roadmap timelines.
- Footer: source/notes, slide number, confidentiality marker.

## Visual language
- White background; **orange** (~#D04A02 / #EB8C00), **coral/rose** (~#E0301E, #DB536A) and dark grey/black (~#2D2D2D) as the core palette; warm light tints for panels.
- Clean sans-serif (Arial/Georgia-like pairing for emphasis); title ~24–28 pt, body 12–14 pt.
- Large statistic callouts in accent color; simple flat icons.
- Charts: columns, bars, stacked bars, doughnuts sparingly; highlight with orange, context in greys and tints; direct labels.
- Divider slides with bold accent color blocks and large numbering.

## Rules of thumb
- Lead each section with a key takeaways box (3 bullets max).
- Back claims with a benchmark or survey statistic and cite the source and sample size.
- Present options with consistent criteria and a clear recommendation.
- Roadmaps show phases, owners, milestones and quick wins; business case shows costs, benefits and timing.
- Keep text concise: phrases, not paragraphs.

## Workflow
1. Clarify objective, audience and whether the deck is a proposal, report-out or workshop.
2. Draft the executive summary and section titles.
3. Build slides (pptx skill for .pptx); check statistics are sourced, takeaways are present and the roadmap matches the recommendations.
