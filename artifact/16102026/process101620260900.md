It has 11 chapters, from the shipyard's working-capital gap through the proposed token, cash-flow waterfall, OJK route and risks to the final recommendation. Two appendices hold a 34-term glossary and 22 references, and there are 10 redrawn charts. The style matches the NRF report: blue cover, chapter banners, and an illustration plus an infographic for every subsection. As before, the PDF came from LibreOffice, so Word's page breaks may shift slightly from the PDF and the contents page.

**Main conclusion: worth piloting, but not ready to approve as written.** The best part of the idea is spending discipline: money goes straight to suppliers only after BKI and the Navy sign off each build milestone. Three things have to be fixed first:

- **Investors' right to be paid first from MoD payments** only holds if the MoD and the Ministry of Finance formally acknowledge the transfer of the receivable.
- **The token runs 720 days, but principal is repaid at ship delivery**, which comes after 60–96 months. The two don't line up.
- **OJK is still drafting its tokenisation rules during 2026**, so the report rates regulatory delay as high risk; the deck rates it low.

**Problems in the deck itself, stated in the report:**
- It gives $500M per vessel, but its own $2,16B for two Scorpène submarines means about $1,08B each.
- Go-live is given as 45 days in one place and 90 days in another.
- It calls the token "not debt", yet it's a fixed-yield debt note.
- The "Standard Chartered $2T by 2028" figure looks like Standard Chartered's stablecoin forecast. Its forecast for tokenised real-world assets is $30,1T by 2034.
- "6 validators tolerate 2 failures" is wrong. Six validators tolerate only one; tolerating two needs seven.
- PT PAL runs 2 of the 4 network nodes, so it can't approve transactions alone, but switching its nodes off would stop the network.

**Recalculated figures:**
- Investor yield is $20,71M (the deck rounds to $21M).
- Against a bank loan at 10,5% plus 2,5% fees, PAL-SUB-1 saves about $14,1M. That's more than the deck's $8–12M, but it leaves out platform, custody and audit costs.
- If Danantara funds itself at the 2% coupon of its Patriot Bond, paying 7% on $150M hands it about $14,8M over 720 days.

**Evidence I added:**
- Market forecasts from BCG × Ripple and Standard Chartered, compared with what is actually tokenised today (about $19,3B in March 2026).
- FSB and BIS warnings on tokenisation risks, and Singapore's Project Guardian trade-finance pilot.
- The ERC-3643 token standard and the Hyperledger Besu network documentation.
- OJK's sandbox rules (POJK 3/2024), the IDDB precedent, and the four sandbox graduates announced in January 2026.
- Bank Indonesia's Digital Rupiah (Proyek Garuda) status, Danantara's assets and Patriot Bond.
- Actual progress on the Scorpène and Merah Putih frigate contracts.

The risk ratings, recalculations and approval conditions are the report's own judgements and are labelled as such. The deck's other claims of prior clearance ("SEC-acknowledged", "pre-cleared by BSSN and OJK") couldn't be verified from public sources.
